once

once is a private app for tracking home poker games among friends: who bought in, who cashed out, and who owes whom at the end of the night.

Effective date: 11 September 2026

Contact: help@oncepoker.app

1who runs once

once is built and operated by an individual developer, not a company. Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent to the contact address above.

2once does not handle money

This is the most important thing to understand about the app. once is a record-keeping tool. It does not process, transfer, hold, or facilitate any payment. It records what players agreed happened at a game and calculates who owes whom.

Players settle up between themselves using their own payment apps, outside of once. If you choose to save a payment handle in your profile, once stores that text so other players can find you in their own payment app. once never initiates a transfer and has no access to your bank, card, or payment app account.

3who can use once

once is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and accounts are not knowingly created for anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact the address above and it will be deleted.

4what once collects

Account information

You sign in with Google. once receives your email address, your name, and your Google profile picture URL. once never receives or stores your Google password.

Information you create in the app

  • Games you host or join, including stakes, start and end times, and chip configuration
  • Buy-ins, add-ons, cash-outs, and the resulting net amounts
  • Settlements between players and whether they have been marked paid
  • A display name and an optional profile photo
  • Optional payment handles you choose to save, so other players can settle with you
  • Posts, comments, likes, mentions, and direct messages between players
  • Images you upload to a post, a message, or your profile
  • Friend requests, groups, and group membership
  • Optional hand records, side-game predictions, and high hand entries

Information created automatically

  • Timestamps for the records above
  • Scheduled in-app reminders about unsettled amounts
  • Standard server logs kept by once's hosting provider, used to operate and secure the service

What once does not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind
  • No location data
  • No contacts, camera roll, or microphone access beyond a photo you deliberately choose to upload
  • No third-party analytics or tracking

5why once uses this information

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to show a game's state to the players in it, to calculate settlements and leaderboards, to deliver the social features, and to remind players about amounts that are still outstanding. once does not use your information to build advertising profiles.

6what other players can see

once is a shared record among people who play together, so some of your information is visible to other players by design.

  • Your display name and profile photo are visible to other players
  • Your buy-ins, cash-outs, and net result for a game are visible to the other players in that game
  • Your posts, comments, and messages are visible to their intended audience
  • A saved payment handle is visible to players you need to settle with, and to friends

Access is enforced at the database level, so players cannot read records from games they were not part of.

7services once relies on

once uses a small number of third-party services, and shares only what each needs to do its job.

  • Google, for sign-in. Google tells once your email, name, and profile picture URL
  • Supabase, which hosts once's database, file storage, and server functions. Data is stored on servers in the United States
  • Apple, which distributes the app. Apple may collect its own information about downloads and crashes under its own privacy policy
  • Venmo, only when you ask once to check that a handle you entered exists. once requests the public profile page for that handle. No account details are sent, and once does not connect to your Venmo account

once does not sell your personal information, and does not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

8how long once keeps your data

Game records are kept for as long as your account exists, because their purpose is to be a lasting history of your games. If you delete your account, your profile, posts, messages, and uploaded images are deleted.

Records of games you played in may be retained in a form that no longer identifies you, so that the other players' own history and settlement records stay accurate. A settled debt cannot be un-recorded for one player without corrupting it for the other.

9your choices and rights

  • You can edit your display name, profile photo, and payment handles at any time
  • You can remove a saved payment handle, which stops it being shown to other players
  • You can delete your account, which deletes the data described above
  • You can ask for a copy of the information once holds about you

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. once does not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. To exercise any of these rights, use the contact address above.

10security

Data is stored with a managed database provider, encrypted in transit, and protected by per-user access rules so that players can only read records they are entitled to see. Sign-in is handled by Google, so once never sees your password. No service can promise perfect security, but these are the measures in place.

11changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your information is used, the effective date above will be updated and the change will be noted in the app. Continuing to use once after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12contact

For any question about this policy, your data, or a deletion request, write to help@oncepoker.app.
